How FairBid Protects Farmers

1

List with Minimum Price

You set the reserve minimum price per unit. Bids below your minimum are automatically rejected by the system.

2

Transparent Competition

Registered buyers submit bids during the live auction round. Outbid tracking ensures prices move strictly upward.

3

Unsold Recovery & Control

If no valid bids are received, compare the local agent's offer with market potential. The decision to reopen or sell is always yours.
