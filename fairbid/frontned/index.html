How FairBid Protects Agricultural Producers

1

List with Minimum Price (MSP)

You set the minimum reserve price per unit. Offers below your minimum are flagged, giving you absolute control over sale decisions.

2

Live Transparent Bidding

Registered buyers submit live bids. Price increments move strictly upward in real time, preventing broker cartels and price suppression.

3

Guaranteed Deal Settlement

Accept winning bids in one atomic transaction. Other pending bids are resolved and sale records are stored immutably in MySQL.
